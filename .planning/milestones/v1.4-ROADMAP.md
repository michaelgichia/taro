## Milestone Summary

**Key Decisions:**
- Semantic recorder `dblClick` is the v1.4 assertion-marker convention.
- Marker proof ordering is locked to role+name, then visible text/value, then form-context fallback.
- Marker conversion remains additive to scenario intent and never replaces happy-path coverage.
- QUAL-02 fails explicitly when detected semantic markers produce zero emitted assertions.

**Issues Resolved:**
- Marker gestures now preserve intent and anchor context instead of collapsing into ordinary click noise.
- Generated marker assertions are now explicit, truthful, and helper-boundary aware.
- Marker conversion coverage and unresolved-marker visibility are now reported at generation time.

**Issues Deferred:**
- Structured machine-readable artifact export for marker coverage and unresolved-marker diagnostics.
- Nyquist VALIDATION.md backfill for phases 17-19.

**Technical Debt Incurred:**
- CLI integration tests still rely on mocked resolver/scanner boundaries for speed and determinism.
- Coverage/reporting remains terminal-first and does not yet emit standalone artifacts.
